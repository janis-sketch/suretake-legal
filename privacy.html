SureTake Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

SureTake is a Watch and iPhone voice recorder with optional on-device transcripts. We do not operate accounts or cloud AI.

Data we process

  • Audio you record and optional transcripts stay on your devices (and transfer between your Watch and iPhone).
  • Transcripts use Apple’s on-device Speech framework when available. No cloud speech service is used by SureTake.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the MVP.
